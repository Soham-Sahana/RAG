
LIBRARY POLICY

The college library is located in the central academic building.
The regular library opening hours are 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
Students must present their college identity card to borrow books.
Students may borrow up to three books for fourteen days.
A late return fine of 5 rupees per book per day applies.
The library is closed on Sundays and official college holidays.
